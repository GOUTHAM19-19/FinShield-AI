3. Rule Transparency

Understand what the demo rules mean.

  • High amount: amount crosses the configured demo threshold.
  • High frequency: many transactions in the selected time window.
  • New recipient: recipient has little or no observed history.
  • Unusual hour: transaction occurs outside the user's typical activity window.
  • Rapid chain: transfers connect accounts within a short time interval.
